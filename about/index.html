About Max

The diagnostic comes before the fix.

I learned that in an ultrasound room long before I applied it to a business.

I grew up in Montgomery County, Maryland, with deep roots in Frederick County. Before I worked in local search, I spent years as a vascular ultrasound technologist at Frederick Health, where every decision started with an accurate measurement. You find what's actually there first. Then you decide what to do about it.

I bring that same habit to everything I do now. For a service business, that means searching the way its customers search before suggesting a single change. For a building owner, it means reading the building's public energy record before anyone talks about equipment.

I'm curious by nature. I'll read the fine print, the county data and the review nobody answered. Then I share what I find in plain language, with the source attached.

"We live in interesting times. All you can do is find the adventure in life."

I write about the systems hiding in plain sight, and what I find in them, on Medium.
