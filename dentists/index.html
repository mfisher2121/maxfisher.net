Built for a practice

Nothing that puts your patients at risk.

I never need patient records. Everything I check is what any member of the public can see.

A common mistake: replying to a review in a way that confirms the reviewer is a patient or mentions their treatment. I'll flag replies like that and suggest wording that's warm without sharing anything private.

You get a short written report with screenshots, and the fixes in order of how many new patients each one is likely costing you. No long contract, and no promise of a number-one ranking.
