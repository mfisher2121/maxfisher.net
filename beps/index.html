For building owners and property managers · Montgomery County, MD

2027 is your decision year.

Montgomery County's Building Energy Performance Standards (BEPS) start checking non-residential buildings of 50,000 square feet or more against their first energy target in 2028. Retrofits take time to plan and pay for. The owners who decide in 2027 have the most choices.

If a building misses its target

What a BPIP is.

At the end of each evaluation period, a covered building either meets its standard or files and begins carrying out a Building Performance Improvement Plan (BPIP). The county says a BPIP must be submitted at least 90 days before the compliance deadline for the standard.

Confirm your building's exact BPIP due date with the county's Department of Environmental Protection before you plan around it: energy@montgomerycountymd.gov.
