Why "market intelligence"

The diagnostic comes before the fix.

Before marketing, I spent years as a vascular ultrasound technologist at Frederick Health. Every decision started with an accurate measurement. Nobody guessed.

Most marketing skips that step. It sells you a package before anyone has looked at what's going wrong. I do it the other way around: I look up your business the way a customer would, read the public data for your area, and tell you plainly what I found.

A real example: a mobile window tinter had reviews saying "couldn't find the location" and "nobody was there." He goes to the customer. Nothing online said so. The fix wasn't more ads. It was one clear sentence: mobile service by appointment, we come to you.
