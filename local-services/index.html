A real example

"Couldn't find the location."

A mobile window tinter had two reviews that stuck with me. One said they couldn't find the location. Another said nobody was there.

He's mobile. He goes to the customer. But nothing on his Google listing or website said so, so people drove to an address and found an empty lot.

The fix wasn't a bigger ad budget. It was making one thing plain everywhere a customer looks:

Mobile service by appointment. We come to you.

Most local businesses have a gap like that. You can't see it from the inside, because you already know how your business works. Your customers don't.

What I check

The places customers decide.

  • Can a first-time customer tell what you do, where you work and how to book?
  • Does your Google Business Profile match how your business actually runs?
  • Do you have a page for the service you want to sell, in the towns you want to sell it?
  • Are you replying to reviews, including the unhappy ones?
  • Can someone book or call in two taps from a phone?
