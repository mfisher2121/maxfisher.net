Why Rockville gets its own check

Rockville is not just another Montgomery County town

The City of Rockville issues its own building and trade permits through its Inspection Services Division, separate from the county. And its housing comes in three waves, each with its own heat pump job.

Postwar

Twinbrook

Small postwar homes, often older ductwork or none, where sizing and duct work decide the job.

1970s to 1980s

Colonials and split-levels

Two-story homes on second or third systems, many still on gas or oil backup.

Late 1990s to 2000s

King Farm and Fallsgrove

Original systems from the build-out are reaching replacement age now.
